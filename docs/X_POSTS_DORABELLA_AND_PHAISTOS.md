## Thread 2: Dorabella — A Musical Sketch, Not a State Secret

**Tagged Accounts**: `@elgarsociety`, `@KlausSchmeh`, `@nickpelling`

### Tweet 1/5 (The Hook / The 127-Year Puzzle) — `273 / 280 chars`
```text
For 127 years, Elgar's Dorabella Cipher (1897) was treated as an English anagram puzzle—producing endless forced readings.

Viktor Wase showed in 2023 that substitution solvers fail on it.

So I (+my agents!) poked around the archives and found something really curious. 🧵👇
```

### Tweet 2/5 (The 1886 Liszt Programme Anchor) — `267 / 280 chars`
```text
Elgar actually used the exact same 24-symbol script 11 years earlier, on an April 1886 Franz Liszt London concert programme.

Back then, Dora Penny was an 11yo schoolchild he had never met.

Seems like this was his personal shorthand/doodle alphabet long before 1897.
```
