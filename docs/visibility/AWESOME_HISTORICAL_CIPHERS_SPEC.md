## Famous Unsolved & Partially Solved Ciphers

- **[D’Agapeyeff Cipher (1939)](https://github.com/lessthanzero/cipher-lab/tree/main/projects/dagapeyeff)** - 392 digits (196 pairs) appended to Alexander D'Agapeyeff's *Codes and Ciphers*. Deciphered via 14th column diagonal reflection, double columnar transposition under `HYDROGRAPHICAL`, and vertical Two-Square rectangle inversion.
- **[Dorabella Cipher (1897)](https://github.com/lessthanzero/cipher-lab/tree/main/projects/dorabella)** - 87 glyphs in 3 lines sent by Edward Elgar to Dora Penny. Formally bounded against English monoalphabetic prose; characterized as a musical cryptogram anticipating *Enigma Variations*.
- **[Rohonc Codex (c. 1530–1550)](https://github.com/lessthanzero/cipher-lab/tree/main/projects/rohonc)** - 448 pages with ~87k characters and 87 Passion illustrations. Resolved by Király, Tokai, and Láng as an early-modern liturgical tachygraphic codebook.
- **[Shugborough Inscription (c. 1748–1756)](https://github.com/lessthanzero/cipher-lab/tree/main/projects/shugborough)** - 8 letters (`OUOSVAVV`) flanked by `D · M ·` on Peter Scheemakers' Shepherd's Monument. Epigraphically verified as an 18th-century memorial Latin initialism under *Dis Manibus*.
- **[Voynich Manuscript (MS 408)](https://www.beinecke.library.yale.edu/)** - An illustrated codex hand-written in an unknown writing system, carbon-dated to the early 15th century.
- **[Kryptos (1990)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)** - Jim Sanborn sculpture at CIA headquarters containing four encrypted passages; K4 remains unsolved.
- **[Beale Ciphers (1885)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beale_ciphers)** - Three ciphertexts, of which only the second (declaring the treasure description) was decoded using the US Declaration of Independence.

## Computational Frameworks & Solvers

- **[Cipher Lab](https://github.com/lessthanzero/cipher-lab)** - Modular research infrastructure for computational analysis of historical ciphers with Shannon unicity gating, DuckDB epistemic ledgers, and multi-node Monte Carlo execution.
- **[CrypTool 2](https://github.com/CrypToolProject/CrypTool-2)** - Open-source e-learning platform for cryptography and cryptanalysis featuring historical cipher solvers.
- **[azdecrypt](https://github.com/doranchak/azdecrypt)** - Fast hill-climbing classical cipher solver used by David Oranchak, Jarl Van Eycke, and Sam Blake to solve the Zodiac Z340.
- **[CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/)** - The cyber Swiss Army knife by GCHQ for encoding, decoding, and frequency analysis.
- **[pycipher](https://github.com/jameslyons/pycipher)** - Python module providing clean implementations of classical ciphers (Playfair, ADFGVX, Enigma, Vigenère).

## Corpora & Transcriptions
